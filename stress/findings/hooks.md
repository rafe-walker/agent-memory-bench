### Method notes
- What a false alarm costs (H2-07): gate setup, then `kl get runbook --actor agent-a` (pending = 0), then pulse `npm ci && chmod +x scripts/deploy.sh` twice → deny, deny.
- Why H2-01 and H2-10 are S1 and not S2: the pulse does not just stay silent. It affirms safety ("none of it blocks") while quoting the change as something the agent "relied on", and kronicle-lite's own `pending` for the right actor returns 1 at that moment.
- Shim cases judge by effect: the bare remote's `main` equals the local HEAD (the push landed), not by exit code.
- H2-17 is unused: the `--on-timeout` behaviour was found to be as documented (see Passed).
- Info only, not filed: a self-recursive `git` wrapper (one that loops even without shims) makes the pulse's own `git` call loop until the 10 s timeout, after which the command runs with the TIMED OUT line; a "read" with its output discarded (`get --actor agent-a >/dev/null`) clears the gate (a read is a read, by design).

### Gaps
- **Shells:** zsh is not installed (only dash and bash were run).
- **Platforms:** Windows `.cmd`/`.ps1` wrappers are not exercised (`shim exec` is checked on Linux only); macOS bash 3.2 / APFS same-second and case-insensitive paths (H2-23 case variant, agent_ids differing only by case) were not run.
- **agent_id collisions:** analysed only. Ids are kept whole when ≤ 64 filename-safe characters and replaced by the first 12 hex characters of a SHA-1 otherwise, so a collision is not practical on a case-sensitive FS.
- **Not run:** a live `claude -p` arm.
- **Pending stale cache:** `pending` clearing through MCP `recall` with truncated snippets (a "read" that never showed the changed text) was not tested; the CLI has no recall command.
